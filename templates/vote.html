How your vote is protected

Post‑Quantum End‑to‑End Verifiable Anonymous
  • Dilithium Signature — proves the vote came from you without revealing your identity.
  • Kyber Encryption — keeps the contents of your vote secret, even against quantum computers.
  • Mixnet Cascade — shuffles encrypted votes so no one can link you to a ballot.
  • Blockchain Layer — each encrypted vote is anchored on an immutable ledger.

After submitting, you will receive a receipt hash. Save it carefully:

  • Use the receipt later to verify that your vote is still present on the blockchain.
  • A mismatch would indicate tampering or deletion.

Never share your private key. Only the receipt hash is meant to be public and can be safely stored or shown to auditors.
